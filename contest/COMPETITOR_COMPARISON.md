# SPARK versus three real alternatives

Reviewed 2026-09-04. Method: primary product documentation and feature walkthroughs, not a timed hands-on benchmark or a claim about all available features. No accounts created or third-party workspaces modified.

Common task: preserve an unfinished observation, invite perspectives, decide one experiment, then return learning with credit.

| Product | Documented workflow for this task | Stronger than SPARK today | SPARK's narrower distinction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Are.na | Add a text/link block to a channel, invite collaborators, connect references, export a channel. | Real collaborators, persistent collections, flexible media, channel privacy and exports; SPARK is one local browser room. | An explicit observation → perspective → test → return workflow and a named capability intention, exposed to browser agents. Are.na could approximate this with conventions; it is not missing shared learning. |
| Loomio | Start a group discussion, gather responses, propose a course of action, record outcome. | Mature group discussion, polling and decision records; SPARK has neither authenticated membership nor collective decision rules. | A low-obligation spark need not become a proposal or vote. The room asks what people become better at as well as what they produce. |
| Kialo Edu | Contribute claims, connect supporting/refuting arguments, cite sources, find duplicates and review source list. | Structured argumentation, duplicate suggestions, source review and author search; SPARK only groups exact cited URLs and does not verify independence. | Lived observation need not first become a defensible claim; the next object is an experiment and a return, not a winning argument. |

## Primary evidence inspected

- [Are.na about and included features](https://www.are.na/about): collaborative channels, multiple media types, export and API support.
- [Are.na channels](https://help.are.na/docs/getting-started/channels): ownership, collaborators and open/closed/private distinctions.
- [Are.na handling blocks with care](https://help.are.na/docs/guides/handling-blocks-with-care): connections retain contextual trails.
- [Loomio getting started](https://www.loomio.com/docs/en/user_manual/overview): groups, discussions, polls and searchable decision records.
- [Loomio proposals](https://www.loomio.com/docs/en/user_manual/polls/proposals): proposal workflow and response choices.
- [Kialo duplicate claims](https://support.kialo-edu.com/en/hc/avoiding-duplicate-claims/): textual similarity suggestions are useful but not exhaustive.
- [Kialo Sources sidebar](https://releases.kialo-edu.com/2021/12/08/sources-sidebar/): sources linked to claims and quotation/notes.
- [Kialo finding claims](https://support.kialo-edu.com/en/hc/finding-a-claim/): author, text and review-state search.

## What this changed in the candidate

These comparisons reinforce preserving context instead of selling an AI fact-checker. R04 keeps earlier contributions available; R09 distinguishes capability intentions from outcomes; R10 makes a portable handoff. Those improvements do not erase the alternatives' real multi-user advantages. The literature review occurred during the campaign and validates this positioning; it is not evidence of user preference or causal impact.

## Competitive judgment

Plausible differentiated prototype, not a defensible predicted winner. WebMCP's shared state and human-reviewed handoff are demonstrable. The largest weakness is absence of a real network: independent people cannot join the same durable room from separate devices. The video/public build must not imply otherwise. No numeric winning odds or invented judge scores.
